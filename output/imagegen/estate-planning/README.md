### Generation prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: portrait editorial photograph for an estate-planning law firm's website, to sit beneath an "Islamic Estate Planning" heading inside a deep burgundy section.
Create a photorealistic, premium estate-plan presentation binder still life. This is a concept product mockup, not a real client's belongings.
Scene: a warm light-oak tabletop in soft natural window light.
Main subject: a beautifully made, substantial closed burgundy leather estate-planning binder/padfolio, with fine natural leather grain, discreet matching stitching, a rounded spine, and a glimpse of neatly organized cream paper edges. The binder should feel like a thoughtful, lasting item a family receives from their attorney.
Cover typography: restrained, elegant gold-foil debossed serif lettering, rendered exactly and clearly on two centered lines. The smaller name line reads "THE BENNETT FAMILY". The larger title line below reads "ESTATE PLAN". These are the only readable words anywhere in the image. The family name is fictional sample personalization. Keep the gold subtle and realistic, integrated into the leather with tiny recessed impressions rather than floating text.
Supporting objects: a small neat stack of unprinted cream papers partly beside the binder, one refined brass fountain pen, and two ordinary house keys on a simple small keyring. Keep these secondary, arranged naturally with generous breathing room.
Composition: vertical 4:5 portrait composition, close editorial product photograph from a gently angled overhead viewpoint so the cover lettering and thickness/spine can both be seen. The entire binder is visible and occupies most of the frame; keep all important details comfortably away from the edges for responsive website display. Clear visual hierarchy, simple composition readable at about 350px wide. No reserved area for overlay text.
Lighting and mood: quiet confidence, warmth, care, permanence; soft directional daylight, gentle shadows, realistic tactile surfaces, natural color and restrained contrast.
Palette: deep burgundy leather approximately #4d1722, cream paper, honey oak, muted brass/gold. A lighter overall scene that contrasts attractively against a dark burgundy website background.
Constraints: no people, no hands, no additional text, no law-firm logo, no watermark, no religious icons or calligraphy, no gavel or scales, no fake legal document writing, no excessive ornamentation or dramatic glossy reflections. Deliver the standalone photograph, not a website mockup, not a collage.
```
